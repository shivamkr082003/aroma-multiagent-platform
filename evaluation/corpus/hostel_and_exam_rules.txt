STUDENT HANDBOOK 2026-27: HOSTEL RESIDENCE RULES AND EXAMINATION REGULATIONS
Document code: SH-2026-HX. Issued by the Office of the Dean of Student Affairs.

PART A — HOSTEL RESIDENCE RULES

A1. Allotment. Hostel rooms are allotted for one academic year at a time. First-year students are placed in double-sharing rooms in Aryabhatta Hall. Senior students may apply for single rooms in Raman Hall; single rooms are allotted by CGPA, highest first, and ties are broken by date of application.

A2. Fees and deposit. The hostel fee for 2026-27 is 68,000 rupees per year, payable in two instalments. A refundable caution deposit of 10,000 rupees is collected at admission and returned within 45 days after the student vacates the room, minus deductions for damage.

A3. Entry timings. The hostel gates close at 10:30 PM on weekdays and at 11:30 PM on Fridays and Saturdays. A student who returns after the gate closes must sign the late-entry register at the security desk. Three late entries in one month lead to a written warning and a notice to the parents or guardian.

A4. Guests. Day visitors are allowed in the common lounge between 9 AM and 7 PM only and must leave a government photo ID at the reception. Overnight guests are not permitted in student rooms under any circumstances.

A5. Electrical appliances. Kettles, induction cooktops, room heaters and irons are not allowed in rooms because of fire risk. Laptops, phone chargers and table lamps are permitted. Any banned appliance found during an inspection will be confiscated and a fine of 2,000 rupees will be charged.

A6. Mess. Breakfast is served from 7:30 AM to 9:30 AM, lunch from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM and dinner from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM. A student who will be away for at least four consecutive days may apply for a mess rebate through the student portal at least two days in advance.

A7. Ragging. Ragging in any form is a criminal offence and leads to immediate expulsion from the hostel. Complaints can be made anonymously to the Anti-Ragging Squad on the 24-hour helpline 1800-180-5522.

PART B — EXAMINATION REGULATIONS

B1. Attendance requirement. A student must have at least 75 percent attendance in a course to sit for its end-semester examination. Students between 65 and 75 percent may appeal to the Head of Department with a medical certificate; below 65 percent there is no appeal and the course must be repeated.

B2. Assessment scheme. Each theory course is graded out of 100 marks: 20 for continuous assessment (quizzes and assignments), 30 for the mid-semester examination and 50 for the end-semester examination. A student must score at least 40 marks overall and at least 20 out of 50 in the end-semester examination to pass.

B3. Conduct in the examination hall. Students must reach the hall 15 minutes before the start time. Entry is refused 30 minutes after the paper begins. Mobile phones, smart watches and any programmable devices must be deposited outside. Using unfair means results in cancellation of the paper and a hearing before the Unfair Means Committee.

B4. Make-up examinations. A student who misses an end-semester examination for a documented medical emergency may take a make-up examination, which is held within three weeks after results are announced. The application must be submitted within 7 days of the missed paper.

B5. Revaluation. A student may apply for revaluation of an end-semester answer book within 10 days of the results being declared, by paying a fee of 500 rupees per course. If the revised marks differ by more than 10 percent, the fee is refunded.

B6. Grade points. Marks are converted to grades as follows: 90 and above is O (10 points), 80 to 89 is A+ (9 points), 70 to 79 is A (8 points), 60 to 69 is B+ (7 points), 50 to 59 is B (6 points), 40 to 49 is P (5 points) and below 40 is F (0 points).
